1. Your six rules
Rule	What the code must check
R1 – Equipment	User has the required equipment.
R2 – Level	Exercise difficulty is not above the user's level.
R3 – Time	Each workout fits the available session time.
R4 – Recovery	Same major muscle isn't trained on consecutive days.
R5 – No repeats	Same exercise isn't repeated in one session.
R6 – Weekly volume	Weekly sets per muscle stay within minimum and maximum limits.
